Second Edition · In Development
The first edition told you
what a museum is.
The second tells you
what it's in.

The operating environment every museum now shares has fundamentally shifted. AI tools in interpretation and collections. Demographic changes in every American city. Federal funding structures in flux. Digital audience systems that didn't exist in 2015.

Programming-driven visitation. The death of the "build it and they will come" assumption. Institutions that exist inside their four walls, and institutions that exist everywhere their audience is.

Museums 101, Second Edition is about the world your museum opens into — and how to plan honestly for it.
